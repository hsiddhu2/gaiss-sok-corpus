# GAISS 2026 SoK — Corpus Inventory

Supplementary material for **"SoK: Generative AI Across the Secure Software Development Lifecycle — Risks, Defenses, and DevSecOps Integration"** (IEEE GAISS 2026).

This repository enumerates the full corpus systematized in the paper so that the framework population and every evidence-tier assignment are independently auditable.

## Method (as reported in the paper)

The corpus is an **expert-curated systematization**, not an exhaustive database-driven systematic review. It was assembled by expert selection over the leading security, software-engineering, and machine-learning venues, **seeded from four recent surveys** and extended by **backward and forward snowballing** from their reference lists and citing papers. Inclusion required peer review at a listed venue (or status as a widely adopted standard), with preference for released artifacts and explicit threat models; one industry report is included where no peer-reviewed equivalent exists and is marked accordingly. We make no completeness claim.

## Corpus at a glance

- **36** total references: **24 empirical security studies** systematized in the framework (**23 peer-reviewed + 1 industry report**), plus **2** foundational model papers, **4** seed surveys, **3** evaluation benchmarks, and **3** governance standards.

- **Span:** 2018–2025.

- **Venue communities (24 studies):** security 14 (USENIX Security 7, IEEE S&P 3, ACM CCS 2, NDSS 1, ACM AISec 1); software engineering 5 (ICSE 2, ISSTA 1, IEEE TSE 1, IEEE SANER 1); machine learning 3 (NeurIPS 2, ICML 1); other IEEE 1 (SMC); industry 1.

- **Evidence tiers (24 studies):** **E1 = 8 (33%)**, **E2 = 16 (67%)**, **E3 = 0**. E3 material enters only as governance guidance (NIST SSDF, OWASP LLM Top 10). **Four framework cells remain open** (no peer-reviewed study found).

- **Tiering:** performed by the author against the paper's rubric (E1 = human-audited or exploit/crash-validated; E2 = analyzer- or benchmark-scored; E3 = single-setting/practitioner), rechecked on a second pass. Single-grader; no inter-rater statistic. Studies spanning tiers are listed at their **primary** validation method.

\* [D11] is an industry engineering report; its real-CVE discoveries are exploit-validated (E1) but it is not peer-reviewed.
